# Notes

The study files are arranged by module, with individual PDFs linked directly from the Paperroom website.

- **PRM/** — Chapters 9, 11, 12, 14 and 15 as PDFs directly inside this folder. No chapter subfolders or combined folder.
- **ITS/** — Chapters 8 through 12.
- **ADT/** — Chapters 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 19.
- **ADP/** — Empty for now; ready for future notes.

These are **study notes**, not question papers. The working online PRM quizzes load their question datasets from `papers/prm/` and `exam-data.js`.

If you rename a PDF, update the `NOTE_FILES` entries in `app.js` so the website's **Read notes** link stays valid.
