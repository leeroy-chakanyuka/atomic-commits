# Notes library

These folders are prepared for you to upload your own study files in the repository. The site does **not** copy or link to your private Google Drive.

- `ADP/`, `ADT/`, `ITS/`: module material
- `PRM/chapter-09/`, `chapter-11/`, `chapter-12/`, `chapter-14/`, `chapter-15/`: PRM chapter notes
- `PRM/combined/`: mixed revision notes

Upload the PDFs you choose into the relevant folder through GitHub. The site links to these folders so a reader can find the documents once added. Don't upload any confidential information or material you don't have permission to redistribute. 

The question papers are available directly as interactive practice sessions; we are **not** adding separate paper PDFs here.
